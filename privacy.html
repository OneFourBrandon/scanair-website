Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ScanAir handles personal information for website inquiries, local drone services, 3D site documentation, and related software tools.

Last updated: May 21, 2026

1. Who We Are

ScanAir provides drone capture, 3D site documentation, project visuals, and drone software services from North Bay, Ontario, Canada. In this policy, "ScanAir", "we", "us", and "our" refer to the operator of this website and related ScanAir services.

For privacy questions, access requests, or correction requests, contact us through the contact page on this website and address the message to the Privacy Officer.

2. Scope

This policy applies to personal information we collect through scanair.ca, contact forms, project inquiries, email or phone communications, local service delivery, and ScanAir software tools that link to this policy.

It does not apply to third-party websites, platforms, or services that we do not own or control, even if they are linked from our website.

3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with ScanAir, we may collect:

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address, phone number, and business name.
  • Project details, such as property location, site access notes, preferred timelines, and requested services.
  • Messages, attachments, quote requests, support requests, and other information you choose to send us.
  • Drone capture and project materials, which may include images, video, 3D reconstructions, measurements, site context, or visible property details.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, approximate location derived from technical signals, referring pages, and website usage data.
  • Commercial records, such as quotes, invoices, payment status, and service history.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific project and you provide it or authorize us to collect it.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information for purposes a reasonable person would consider appropriate, including to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotes.
  • Plan, schedule, perform, and document drone capture or 3D reconstruction services.
  • Assess project feasibility, site access, safety, airspace, weather, and operational requirements.
  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve our website and software tools.
  • Send service messages, project updates, support responses, invoices, and administrative notices.
  • Send commercial electronic messages only where permitted by consent, an existing relationship, or applicable law.
  • Maintain business records, prevent misuse, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

5. Consent

We collect, use, and disclose personal information with your consent, unless otherwise permitted or required by law. Consent may be express or implied depending on the context, such as when you submit a contact form or request a quote.

You may withdraw consent where legally available. Withdrawing consent may limit our ability to provide services, respond to requests, or complete an active project.

6. Drone Capture and Property Privacy

Drone capture can record property conditions, vehicles, signage, people, neighbouring properties, or other contextual details. We aim to limit capture to what is reasonably necessary for the requested project and to operate in a manner that respects privacy, property access, and applicable aviation and trespass rules.

Clients are responsible for ensuring they have the authority to request capture of a site and for notifying us of privacy-sensitive areas, access restrictions, or safety concerns before work begins.

7. Disclosure and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose personal information to service providers and advisors that help us operate the website, host files, deliver email, provide storage, process payments, manage business records, obtain insurance, or provide professional advice.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect safety, to respond to legal process, to enforce agreements, or as part of a business transaction such as a sale, merger, or reorganization.

8. Storage and Cross-Border Processing

Personal information may be stored or processed in Ontario, elsewhere in Canada, or in other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. When information is processed outside Canada, it may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction.

We use contractual, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information handled by service providers.

9. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service delivery, support, business records, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and security. Retention periods vary based on the type of information and the project or legal context.

10. Safeguards

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. No website, email system, cloud service, or data transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use essential technical storage needed for site operation, security, performance, or embedded services. If we add optional analytics, advertising pixels, or similar tracking tools, we will configure them in a way intended to comply with applicable Canadian requirements and update this policy as needed.

12. Access, Correction, and Questions

You may request access to personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or ask questions about our privacy practices. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or another applicable privacy authority.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
