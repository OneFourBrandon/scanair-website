§ 01 Purpose

A practical visual record for roof decisions.

Drone inspection capture gives you a safer way to review visible roof conditions, document changes, and brief the right people before anyone needs ladders, lifts, or repeated site visits.

Our work supports inspection, quoting, maintenance, insurance, and planning conversations. It is not a structural, engineering, or certified roofing assessment unless that scope is separately agreed with a qualified professional.

§ 02 Capture

What we can document from the air.

We focus on clear, useful roof and exterior context rather than generic flyover media.

  1. 01

    Roof surface condition

    Visible shingle wear, staining, debris, missing material, ponding indicators, and broad condition changes.

  2. 02

    Edges, gutters, and drainage

    Eaves, gutters, downspouts, valleys, transitions, snow guards, and drainage paths that are hard to review from grade.

  3. 03

    Flashing and roof penetrations

    Chimneys, vents, skylights, roof-wall interfaces, pipe boots, and areas commonly flagged for closer review.

  4. 04

    Site and access context

    Neighbouring constraints, slope, access limitations, material staging areas, and exterior conditions around the building.

§ 03 Workflow

Simple capture, cleaner follow-up.

We plan the flight around the property, lighting, weather, privacy, and the specific roof areas you need documented.

  1. Scope Confirm property, areas of concern, timing, access notes, and intended use.
  2. Capture Fly a structured roof and exterior pass with safety, privacy, and airspace checks.
  3. Deliver Provide organized imagery and optional annotated visuals for contractors or stakeholders.
